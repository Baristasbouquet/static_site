Plant Swap!
Urbandale Location

Sunday 8/22 from 8am - 2pm

Got some plants, clippings, or seeds itching for a new home? Or perhaps you've got a plant that's flopped and needs a superhero to save it? Swing by the Urbandale Barista's Bouquet on Saturday, August 22nd from 8am-2pm for our Plant Swap!

You can bring as many as your green thumb desires, but we recommend the golden rule: Bring 1 plant + Take 1 plant, so everyone leaves with a leafy new friend! This event is FREE to attend. Sip on some coffee while you mingle with your fellow plant enthusiasts! 🌿☕️
